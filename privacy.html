隐私政策 Privacy Policy — HaiTianXueXian （海天雪线）

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 19 日 · Last updated: November 19, 2026

海天雪线是一款大温哥华滑雪信息应用。本应用不收集任何个人数据。 HaiTianXueXian is a ski-conditions app for the Greater Vancouver area. This app collects no personal data.

我们不收集什么 What we do not collect

  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、位置、设备标识或任何账号信息；本应用没有账号系统。 We collect no names, email addresses, phone numbers, locations, device identifiers, or account information. There is no account system.
  • 不使用追踪器，不投放广告，不向第三方出售或分享任何数据。 We use no trackers, serve no ads, and do not sell or share any data with third parties.

天气数据 Weather data

打开应用时，应用会向 Open-Meteo 请求公开天气预报（各雪场山腰海拔的气温、降雪与冻结层高度），用于显示雪况。请求中不附带任何个人数据，Open-Meteo 不会收到可以识别你的信息。 When you open the app, it requests public weather forecasts from Open-Meteo (temperature, snowfall, and freezing level at each resort's mid-mountain elevation) to display snow conditions. No personal data is sent with these requests, and Open-Meteo receives nothing that can identify you.

打包清单 Packing checklist

打包清单的勾选状态只保存在你自己的设备上（本次会话内），关闭应用后即清除，不会上传到任何服务器。 Packing-checklist ticks are stored only on your own device (for the current session) and are cleared when you close the app. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

外部链接 External links

应用内有指向各雪场官网与 DriveBC 路况的链接。点开后将离开本应用，适用对方网站自己的隐私政策。 The app links to resort websites and DriveBC road conditions. Opening them leaves this app, and their own privacy policies apply.

儿童 Children

本应用不针对 13 岁以下儿童收集任何信息，因为我们本来就不收集任何信息。 This app collects no information from children under 13, because it collects no information at all.

政策变更 Changes

如果本政策将来有变，我们会在应用内更新本页并注明新的更新日期。 If this policy changes in the future, we will update this page in the app and note the new date.
